1. High-Level Description

This application is a database and management tool for a small farm owner whose local farming business is growing. The owner now operates multiple farms and needs one database that covers the whole business instead of a separate one for each farm.

The database tracks what each farm has and what each farm does. It records the buildings, equipment, animals, and workers at each farm, along with the crops planted in each field and their harvests. It also covers the business side: the products each farm has in stock, the orders customers place for those products, the bills each farm owes to its vendors, and the payments made toward those bills.

The owner, farm managers, and other workers use a web application to look up this information and to keep it current. With everything in one place, the owner can compare farms and see the state of the whole business at once.
